# Acme Tech — Employee Handbook (2026)

## Working hours
Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Employees may work from home up to 3 days per week.

## Leave
- Paid annual leave: 24 days per year.
- Sick leave: 12 days per year; a medical certificate is needed after 2 consecutive days.
- Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year.

## Equipment
Every engineer receives a laptop worth ₹85,000, refreshed every 3 years. A monthly internet
allowance of ₹1,500 is paid with salary.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a yearly learning budget of ₹40,000 for courses and certifications.
Unused budget does not carry forward.
